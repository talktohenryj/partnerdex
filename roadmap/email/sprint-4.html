Sprint 4 — Lifecycle email (v2/v3)

Not designed · repo: partnerdex · open questions only

Henry, 2026-10-09: "No lifecycle for v1. That can be a v2 or 3." This page is not a Build list. It holds the questions that must be answered before anyone builds automated email, so they are not lost and not invented later.

What "lifecycle" means here

An email that sends itself to one person when a condition becomes true for them — not a broadcast Henry presses Send on. Candidate triggers raised on 2026-10-09:

Trial started / trial endingsubscriptions.trial_status, trial_started_at, trial_ends_atTrial end date per (app, shop) must be reliable to the day

Open questions (each changes schema or API shape)

  1. Trigger source. Hang triggers off the existing seams — the sync's onSyncComplete listener and customer_events (the way src/notifications/dispatch.ts already finds new events for Slack) — or evaluate conditions on a clock ("every hour, who is now 5 days into a trial")? Event-driven reuses notifications; clock-driven is the only way to do "no login for 30 days".
  2. One send per person per trigger, ever? Needs an email_automation_sends(automation_id, email, shop_id, sent_at) uniqueness rule. What resets it — a reinstall, a new trial?
  3. Frequency cap across everything. If a person qualifies for a broadcast and two automations in one week, who wins? A global "max N emails per person per 7 days" needs a check inside the S2 runner too.
  4. Sequences or single emails? "Day 1, day 3, day 6" is a sequence with exit conditions (they paid → stop). That is a much bigger model than single triggered emails. Recommendation: v2 = single emails only.
  5. Quiet hours / time zone. Shops have no reliable time zone in PartnerDex. Send in REPORTING_TIMEZONE business hours?
  6. Consent for automated email to staff and collaborators. Broadcasts let Henry choose roles each time. An automation runs unattended — should it default to owners only?
  7. Kill switch. One toggle that pauses all automations (and is it per automation as well)?
  8. Upstream fit. Is automation generic enough to go upstream, or fork-only?

What v1 already gives v2

  • The audience resolver (S1) — an automation's "who" is the same definition plus a trigger.
  • The renderer, transport, unsubscribe, and runner (S2) — an automation enqueues email_sends rows under a campaign-like parent and the same runner sends them.
  • Delivery events and suppression (S3) — unchanged.

Final vision

After v1 (S1–S3) and a v2 lifecycle layer: one Email section in PartnerDex where every message to a merchant — broadcast or automatic — is chosen by store status and role, sent from Henry's own address, honours every unsubscribe and bounce in one suppression store, and shows up on that person's contact row next to their store's MRR.
