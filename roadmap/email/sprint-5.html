Sprint 5 — App messages (weekly recap first)

Not started · repos: partnerdex + iziGift · branch email/s5-messages off email-upstream · migration 8 · after S4

Why this ships now

Henry wants a weekly email that tells each merchant how the week went: gift cards redeemed, the extra spend, cashback, cards about to expire, and one thing to do next. The numbers live in iziGift today. Usage Metrics will bring them into PartnerDex, but that takes time. This sprint lets an app send PartnerDex the data for one shop and a template name; PartnerDex fills in a template Henry edits in the dashboard and sends it to that shop's owners and staff, with the preference page, the footer, and bounce handling from S3 and S4. Decided 2026-11-04 (Henry): the app sends data only, not finished HTML.

Why this is independently deployable

One migration with two new tables and one new column, one new ingest route, one new tab. Campaigns, the runner, and the webhook are reused, not changed. A template starts as Draft, and the route refuses to send for a Draft template, so deploying this sprint sends nothing until Henry sets a template Live and iziGift starts calling. When S6 ships later, the same template can be filled from Usage Metrics on a schedule instead; the template does not change.
